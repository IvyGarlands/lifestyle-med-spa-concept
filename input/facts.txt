7. REGULATED-VERTICAL CONSTRAINTS APPLIED TO THIS BUILD
================================================================================

a. PHI / forms. Their site advertises HIPAA compliance. Our ContactForm posts
   to a third-party form relay with no business-associate agreement. The form
   therefore collects NAME, PHONE, EMAIL and PREFERRED TIME only, and carries
   a visible notice inside the <form> telling people not to send medical
   details. No weight field, no goal weight, no medication field, no condition
   field.

b. Weight-loss claims are FTC-enforced. NO before/after imagery. NO pound
   figures anywhere on the page — including the founder's own published
   22 lb / four months, which is omitted by choice. No timelines, no "typical
   results", no "guaranteed", no "sustainable results" framed as a promise.
   Their word "sustainable" appears only inside a directly attributed quote.

c. Drug names are third-party trademarks. Mounjaro, Ozempic, Wegovy and
   Zepbound appear only inside a factual list of medications the practice
   works with, carrying a trademark note. Never a headline, never a logo,
   never implied endorsement.

d. Provider claims. "Board-certified" is used ONLY attached to the named
   provider and the named board — Michael Rankin, MD, "a diplomate of the
   American Board of Internal Medicine", verbatim. Kristina Dino is described
   as "dual board-certified" because that is their word for her, in her own
   bio, and the two boards are named in her credential string.

e. Reviews naming staff members are quoted verbatim and un-edited except for
   surname reduction.
