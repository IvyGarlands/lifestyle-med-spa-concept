## Best channel, ranked

**1. Instagram DM to @lifestylemedspa.** My recommendation.

Their Instagram is the one channel I can prove a human checks — active as of
1 July 2026, actively curated with highlights, link in bio maintained. A DM to
a 598-follower account gets read by whoever runs it, which given the team page
is most likely Samantha Nunley (Business Development & Patient Experience) or
Kim Barrus himself. It is also the lowest-friction way for them to reply badly,
which sounds like a downside and is not: a two-word "not interested" from a
real person is worth more than silence from a real inbox.

**2. Email to info@lifestylehealthservices.com.**

It is a real Microsoft 365 mailbox and it is the only address they publish. The
risk is that `info@` at a medical practice is a patient-enquiry inbox, likely
watched by front-desk staff and triaged for appointments. Your message is not
an appointment, so it can die there. Send it anyway as the second touch, and
write the subject so a receptionist can see it is not a patient matter and
forwards it rather than deletes it.

**3. Text to (805) 391-2314.**

They publish this line and invite texting on it, so it is not an intrusion. But
it is almost certainly monitored for patient scheduling, and a sales text into a
clinical line is the fastest way to annoy someone. Use it only if the DM and the
email both go nowhere, keep it to four sentences, and never send it twice.

**4. Phone call to (818) 206-3066.** Last resort.

They are open 28 hours a week across four days. Whoever answers is doing
reception for a live clinic. Cold-calling that line interrupts patient care.
I would not, unless they have already replied and asked you to.

**Do not use Facebook.** I could not verify the page is monitored, and an
unanswered DM there tells you nothing.

## Best time to make contact

Their published hours:

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday | 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM |
| Tuesday | 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM |
| Wednesday | 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM |
| Thursday | 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM |
| Friday, Saturday, Sunday | Closed |

**Send Tuesday or Thursday morning, around 9:30–10:30 AM.**

The reasoning is specific to how they operate. Monday and Wednesday are
four-hour days, so those mornings are compressed and every minute is clinical.
Tuesday and Thursday are their only full days, which means the first hour has
some slack in it before the afternoon fills. Friday through Sunday they are
closed, so anything landing after Thursday afternoon sits unread for four days
and arrives stale on Monday morning into a half-day.

Avoid late Thursday afternoon for the same reason — it is the last hour of
their week.

If you are DMing rather than emailing, the same window applies but it matters
less; Instagram gets checked outside business hours in a way that `info@` does
not.
